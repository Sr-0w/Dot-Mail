Dot Mail

Your mail. Nothing else.

Dot Mail is a free, open-source email app for Android. It connects your phone directly to your email provider (Gmail, Infomaniak, Fastmail, iCloud, Posteo, mailbox.org or your own server) over IMAP and SMTP.

  • No Dot Mail account and no servers in between: your mail stays between your phone and your provider.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Tracking pixels and remote images are blocked by default.
  • Unified inbox, fast full-text search, scheduled send, offline reading, OpenPGP and S/MIME.
  • Sign in with a password, an app password, or "Continue with Google" for Gmail.
