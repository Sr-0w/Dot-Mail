<b>Your mail. Nothing else.</b>

Dot Mail is a free, native Android email client built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Connect directly to standard IMAP/SMTP providers without a Dot Mail account or intermediary service.

<b>Mail that fits your day</b>
<ul>
<li>Multiple accounts, a unified inbox, conversations or individual messages</li>
<li>Folders, stars, snooze, newsletters, filters and configurable swipe actions</li>
<li>Full-content local search with operators and server search for uncached messages</li>
<li>Rich-text drafts, signatures, sender identities and optional SimpleLogin/addy.io alias tools</li>
<li>Undo send, scheduled delivery and an outbox that asks before retrying uncertain sends</li>
<li>Attachments, PDF preview, calendar invitations and optional OpenPGP through OpenKeychain</li>
<li>S/MIME signing and encryption with your own certificate from Android’s certificate store</li>
<li>Per-account download policies, storage limits and optional offline downloads; cached content stays available without a connection</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy and control</b>
<ul>
<li>No ads, analytics, crash reporting or Google Play Services</li>
<li>Sanitized HTML with JavaScript disabled; remote resources blocked by default</li>
<li>Optional sender pictures and remote-image permissions remain under your control</li>
<li>Credentials encrypted with Android Keystore; app data excluded from cloud backup</li>
<li>Optional app lock, notification privacy and hiding content in Recents</li>
<li>Periodic sync, foreground updates and optional instant notifications with an explicit battery trade-off</li>
</ul>

Interface in English, French and German, with light, dark and OLED themes.

<b>Prerelease status</b>
Password/app-password access is available for compatible servers. Gmail browser sign-in is currently limited to listed Google test users, with grants expiring after 7 days until restricted-scope verification. It requires this build’s registered Google client ID. A Gmail app password is an alternative where the account permits it. Microsoft/Outlook OAuth sign-in is not included in the first release. Server settings and provider policies may also restrict access.

JMAP account setup is not included in this release. OpenPGP needs the optional OpenKeychain app. Only downloaded content is available offline. Dot Mail is still a prerelease: keep important mail on your server and report reproducible issues.
