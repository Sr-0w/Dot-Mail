<p>Your mail. Nothing else.</p>

<p><strong>Dot Mail</strong> is a calm, fast, fully native Android email client that talks straight to your mail provider — no account with us, no servers in between, no ads, no tracking. Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later), forever.</p>

<h3>What it does</h3>
<ul>
<li>Multiple accounts with a unified inbox and conversation threads</li>
<li>Read, star, archive, delete, mark spam, move — on one message or many, with configurable swipe actions</li>
<li>Offline-first: everything you've synced is readable without a connection; actions queue and replay</li>
<li>Search across all accounts, locally and on the server, with operators: from:, to:, subject:, has:attachment, is:unread, is:starred</li>
<li>Outbox you can trust: undo send, scheduled send, and no silent duplicates — if a connection dies mid-send, Dot Mail asks before retrying</li>
<li>Plain-text and HTML mail, attachments (open or share with any app), quoted replies folded away</li>
<li>Automatic server detection for the big providers (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, iCloud, Infomaniak, Fastmail, GMX, Posteo, Zoho, …) and any standard IMAP/SMTP server by hand</li>
<li>Works as your default mail app (mailto: links); share files into Dot Mail from other apps</li>
</ul>

<h3>Privacy by construction</h3>
<ul>
<li>No middleman: Dot Mail connects directly to your server over TLS. There is no Dot Mail server.</li>
<li>No analytics, no crash reporters, no ads, no Google Play Services. Not "opt-out" — absent.</li>
<li>Remote images and tracking pixels are blocked by default; you can load them per message.</li>
<li>Mail HTML is sanitised, then rendered with JavaScript, file access and network access disabled.</li>
<li>Before you leave the app on a link, you see where it really goes.</li>
<li>Passwords are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore; app data is excluded from cloud backup.</li>
<li>Optional app lock (fingerprint / face / PIN) and "hide content in Recents".</li>
</ul>

<h3>Status</h3>
<p>Dot Mail is young: it is usable and tested, but not yet battle-hardened on thousands of mailboxes. Password, app-password and Google sign-in work today. Microsoft accounts (Outlook, Hotmail, Live) are not supported, and S/MIME is not implemented yet. Background sync runs every 15 minutes plus live (IDLE) updates while the app is open.</p>

<p>100% Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, no cross-platform layer. Dot Mail is the first of a planned family of independent, privacy-respecting "Dot" apps sharing one design language.</p>
