First public release of Dot Mail.

- IMAP/SMTP with password and app passwords; automatic server detection for major providers
- Unified inbox, conversation threads, folders, local and server search
- Offline-first: cached mail, queued actions that replay, undo send and scheduled send
- HTML mail sanitised and rendered safely; remote images blocked by default
- Notifications with Mark read / Archive actions; optional app lock and hidden Recents
- No telemetry, no accounts with us, no Play Services
